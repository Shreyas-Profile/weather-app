Why this is different

Most weather apps show you one number — the output of one model, or a hidden blend of a few, presented as if it were the truth. This app queries multiple independent weather models at your exact GPS location and shows you where they agree and where they disagree. When forecasts are consistent, confidence is high. When models diverge — usually near storms, fronts, or in complex terrain — confidence drops, and you should trust the forecast less.

Models used: GFS (USA), ECMWF IFS (Europe — often considered the world's best), ICON (Germany), GEM (Canada), MetNo Nordic. Data via the free Open-Meteo API. No sensor fusion yet — coming in v2.

This app needs your location to fetch weather for your spot.
